Research

My PhD project studies the centromere paradox: centromeres perform a deeply conserved chromosome-segregation function, yet their DNA sequence and chromatin environment can evolve rapidly.

Core question

How do centromeres evolve while maintaining function?

I combine comparative genomics, phylogenetics and epigenomic thinking to study how centromere architecture changes across eukaryotic lineages.

Approach

Wet lab meets bioinformatics

I use computational pipelines, genome annotation, long-read data and comparative analyses while staying close to the biological system.

Motivation

Evolution as a lens for genome biology

Centromeres offer a powerful model for understanding how essential genome functions persist through rapid molecular change.
